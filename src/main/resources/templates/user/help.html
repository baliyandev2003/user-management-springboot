Frequently Asked Questions

To reset your password:

  1. Go to Settings → Security tab
  2. Click on "Change Password"
  3. Enter your current password
  4. Enter and confirm your new password
  5. Click "Update Password"

You can update your profile information in two ways:

  • Quick Update: Go to Dashboard → Click on "Edit Profile" button
  • Detailed Update: Go to Settings → General tab → Update all fields

If you forget your username:

  1. Go to the login page
  2. Click on "Forgot Username?" link
  3. Enter your registered email address
  4. Check your email for username recovery instructions
  5. If you don't receive an email, contact support

You can contact our support team through:

  • Email: support@usermanagement.com
  • Phone: +1 (555) 123-4567
  • Live Chat: Available 24/7 on our website
  • Ticketing System: Submit a ticket through the Support Portal
